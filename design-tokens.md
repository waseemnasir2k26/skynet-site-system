## Font whitelist (Google Fonts)

Display: Inter · Poppins · Manrope · Playfair Display · Montserrat · Lora · Space Grotesk · DM Sans · **Instrument Serif** · **Fraunces** · **Cormorant Garamond** · Anton · Bebas Neue · Italiana · Tenor

Body: Inter · DM Sans · Lato · Manrope (rule: serif display + sans body, NOT serif on serif)

## Spacing system

8pt base. Margin scale: 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 128 / 196.

## Type scale (display)

Mobile: H1 64px, H2 40px, H3 28px, body 17px / line-height 1.6
Desktop: H1 96-196px (display), H2 64px, H3 40px, body 17px / 1.6

H1 196px italic only for editorial/luxury (Vow Sanctuary). Skip for trades.

## Accent rule

ONE primary accent per site. Two max. NEVER three (Wellness Funnel was multi but uses cream as glue).

## Don'ts

- ❌ Navy + bright gold ≠ luxury (corporate gala only)
- ❌ Cormorant Garamond ≠ modern wedding (too old-money)
- ❌ Pure white background for medical (clinical, sterile, not warm)
- ❌ Random palette via AI variant — always pick from table OR derive from competitor research
- ❌ Three serifs on one page (Instrument + Fraunces + Cormorant = chaos)
