## Patterns observed across 60 sites

### Stack distribution

- **React + Vite** — 14 sites (fastest dev, marketing)
- **Next.js 14+ / 15 / 16** — 8 sites (SSR, SEO, e-commerce)
- **Static HTML + Tailwind CDN** — 5 sites (deliberate simplicity)
- **WordPress theme + companion** — 3 (sKYNETJOE THEME, hepatologia, atif-malik)
- **Generated monorepo** — 20 (skynet-100-demos)

### Most-used palette tokens

- **Navy / dark blue** — 60% (`#11112e`, `#00273d`, `#1F3A5F`, `#0A2540`, obsidian)
- **Gold / champagne** — 45% (`#D4AF37`, `#D4B895`, `#C9A227`, `#C8A579`)
- **Sage / forest green** — 20% (`#8FB89A`, `#0B5D3B`, `#2D4A3E`)
- **Cream / off-white** — 70% (universal background)
- **Single accent pop** — claret, electric red, mint, terracotta (niche-tied)

### Most-used font pairs

1. **Fraunces + Inter** — 5 sites (wellness, luxury, editorial)
2. **Inter + Space Grotesk** — 4 sites (modern SaaS, lifestyle)
3. **Cormorant Garamond + Inter** — 3 sites (medical, classic luxury)
4. **Instrument Serif + Inter** — 2 sites (modern wedding/luxury)
5. **DM Sans + Playfair Display** — 1 site (finance old-money)
6. **Satoshi + Inter** — 1 site (medical premium)
7. **Anton/Bebas + Inter** — 1 site (indie poster)

### Hero pattern distribution

## Anti-patterns caught + fixed (lessons)

- ❌ Same testimonial across 20 niches (Skynet 20-niche v1) → unique 60 testimonials in v2
- ❌ Picsum photos (Wave 4 caught) → Unsplash CDN per niche
- ❌ Gradient-shader R3F blob (uncanny) → 5 mesh specs per niche
- ❌ Navy + bright gold for wedding (read fintech) → warm beige + claret
- ❌ Cormorant Garamond for modern wedding (too old-money) → Instrument Serif
- ❌ 16 scope Qs (GutReno) → reduce to 5-8
- ❌ Batch SEO publish (SpamBrain Feb 2026) → drip 3-5/week
- ❌ Photo "by eye" → always rotation log

```

  → Static HTML + Tailwind CDN + vanilla JS
```
