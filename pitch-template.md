# Pitch Template — Verbatim Structure

Validated 2026-04-22 (Clinic Pitch). Used 6+ times since w/ ~70% reply rate.

## Anatomy

```
[TRIGGER WORD if Upwork-required]

Hey [Name or "there"] —

[1 SENTENCE HOOK tying to competitor gap or brief signal]

Built a working concept so you can see exactly what I mean:

→ Live: [demo URL]
→ Repo: [github URL]

What's built:
• [bullet 1 — using THEIR vocabulary]
• [bullet 2 — using THEIR vocabulary]
• [bullet 3]
• [bullet 4]
• [bullet 5]

Brand + copy is placeholder. To finish + ship to your domain, I'd need:

1. [Scope Q 1]
2. [Scope Q 2]
3. [Scope Q 3]
4. [Scope Q 4]
5. [Scope Q 5]

Bonus: [pricing-lever Q] — changes fee.

Reply with what you have — live on your domain inside the week.

— [Your name]
[Your role / agency]
[Optional: Phone / WhatsApp]
```

## Reply cadence (no answer)

| Days silent | Action |
|---|---|
| 24h | Soft bump — "wanted to make sure this didn't get buried" |
| 72h | Loom walkthrough top 2 picks (5 min max) |
| 7d | Final close-out — "if timing isn't right, no worries — leaving the demo live, reusing as portfolio" |

## What NOT to do

- ❌ "I hope this email finds you well"
- ❌ "I have 10+ years of experience in..."
- ❌ Long agency intro before the demo link
- ❌ Quoting fee in first message
- ❌ Generic "I can help with your project"
- ❌ Attaching a PDF instead of live URL
